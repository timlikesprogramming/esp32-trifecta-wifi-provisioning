iOS / Unsupported Browser

Web Bluetooth is not supported in this browser (e.g. Safari / iOS).

To set up via SoftAP Captive Portal:

  1. Open Wi-Fi Settings on your mobile device.
  2. Connect to MyDevice_Setup network.
  3. Follow the pop-up portal or navigate to http://192.168.4.1.
Alternative: Press the physical BOOT button on your device during setup to initiate a 120s WPS router search.
